XConnect Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

At XConnect, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, password
  • Profile Information: Profile photo, business name (for vendors), shop address
  • Location Data: GPS coordinates for finding nearby print shops
  • Payment Information: Processed via Cashfree (we don't store card details)
  • Documents: Files you upload for printing (temporarily stored, auto-deleted)
  • Communication: Messages, feedback, and dispute information

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Orders placed, searches, interactions, preferences
  • Location: Approximate location based on IP address
  • Log Data: Server logs, error reports, performance metrics

2. How We Use Your Information

2.1 To Provide Services

  • Connect you with nearby print shops
  • Process and manage orders
  • Send order updates and notifications
  • Verify your identity (email OTP)
  • Facilitate payments via Cashfree

2.2 To Improve Our Platform

  • Analyze usage patterns and trends
  • Fix bugs and improve performance
  • Develop new features
  • Personalize your experience

2.3 To Communicate With You

  • Order status notifications (push, in-app)
  • Marketing communications (only with consent)
  • Support responses
  • Important updates about Terms or Privacy Policy

3. Information Sharing

3.1 With Vendors

When you place an order, we share with the vendor:

  • Order details (what to print, quantity, page count)
  • Pickup token for order identification
  • Your phone number (for order coordination only)
  • Your first name (for communication)
We do NOT share: Your payment details, full address, or email with vendors.

3.2 With Service Providers

  • Cashfree: Payment processing
  • Cloudinary: File storage
  • Resend: Transactional emails
  • Cloudflare: Security and CDN
  • Analytics services: Anonymous usage statistics

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by:

  • Law or legal process
  • Government requests
  • To prevent fraud or abuse
  • To protect our rights or safety

4. Data Storage & Security

4.1 Storage

  • Database: MongoDB (encrypted at rest)
  • Files: Cloudinary with secure URLs
  • Transit: SSL/TLS encryption

4.2 Security Measures

  • Password hashing (bcrypt)
  • JWT access tokens (15-minute expiry)
  • Secure refresh token rotation
  • Rate limiting
  • Helmet.js security headers
  • Regular security audits

4.3 Data Retention

Retention Periods:
• Account data: Until account deletion
• Order data: 2 years for legal compliance
• Wallet transactions: 7 years (financial records)
• Files: Auto-deleted 24hr after order (unless "keep" selected)

5. Your Rights

5.1 Access & Correction

  • View your profile data anytime
  • Update name, phone, email, avatar
  • Request data export

5.2 Deletion

  • Delete account → all personal data removed (except legal requirements)
  • Files auto-deleted after order completion
  • Request deletion of specific data

5.3 Preferences

  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Disable push notifications
  • Turn off location (limited functionality)
  • Manage cookie preferences

6. Cookies & Tracking

6.1 We Use

  • Essential cookies: Login, session, security
  • Functional cookies: Preferences, settings
  • Analytics cookies: Anonymous usage statistics

6.2 Third-Party Cookies

  • Cloudflare (security)
  • Google Analytics (anonymous usage)

7. Children's Privacy

XConnect services are for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the data immediately.

8. Data Transfers

Data is primarily stored on servers in India. When using third-party services, data may be transferred internationally per their privacy policies (all providers comply with applicable data protection laws).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. For significant changes, we will:

  • Send email notification 30 days before
  • Update the "Last Updated" date
  • Show prominent notice in the app

Continued use of XConnect after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

For users in the European Union (GDPR) or California (CCPA):

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to delete your data ("Right to be Forgotten")
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to object to processing

Contact us at privacy@xconnect.app for any data subject requests.
